Bell Timer v1.0

This guide shows how to set the date, the time and the school bells. Every step uses only the three buttons on the timer.

  • 15 bells
  • 24-hour clock
  • Choose days Mon–Sun
  • Ring time 0–239 seconds or minutes
  • 1 bell output (relay A)

The buttons

The timer has three buttons. Each button does one job when you move between screens, and another job when you change a value.

ButtonMoving between screensChanging a value (cursor is shown)
MENUOn the home screen: opens the menu.
On a menu screen: starts changing values.		Goes to the next value. After the last value, the cursor goes away and changing is finished.
− (left)Goes to the previous screen. On the Date screen it saves and goes home.Makes the number smaller, or turns a * on/off.
+ (right)Goes to the next screen. On the last screen it saves and goes home.Makes the number bigger, or turns a * on/off.

Tip. Hold + or − down to change a number fast.

2

Home screen

When you switch on the timer, it shows DJ Tech / Bell Timer v1.0 for 5 seconds. Then the home screen opens.

Bells ring only while the home screen is shown. They do not ring while the menu is open.

3

How the menu works

The menu is a list of screens. Press + to go forward. Press − to go back.

Home→Date→Time→ Bell 01: time→Bell 01: days→Bell 01: duration→Bell 01: output→ Bell 02 …→ … →Bell 15: output→Home (saved)

There are 62 screens: Date, Time, then 4 screens for each of the 15 bells.

  1. Go to the screen you want with + or −.
  2. Press MENU. A small blinking line (the cursor) shows under the first value.
  3. Press + or − to change it.
  4. Press MENU to go to the next value. Keep pressing until the cursor goes away.
  5. Now + and − move between screens again.

Important. When the cursor is shown, + and − change the value. They do not move to another screen. Press MENU until the cursor goes away first.

4

Set the date

  1. On the home screen, press MENU. You see Date.
  2. Press MENU. The cursor is under the day. Change it with + / − (1–31).
  3. Press MENU. Change the month (1–12).
  4. Press MENU. Change the year. Only 2 digits: 26 means 2026.
  5. Press MENU. The cursor is after the day name. Choose the right weekday (Mon … Sun).
  6. Press MENU once more. The cursor goes away.
  7. Press − to save and go home. Or press + to go on to the Time screen.

Set the weekday yourself. The timer does not find the weekday from the date. The bells use the weekday to know if they should ring. If the weekday is wrong, the bells ring on the wrong days.

Example: 20.11.26 is a Friday, so choose Fri.

Tip. The timer allows day 31 in every month. Check that the day is real for that month (for example, no 30 February).

5

Set the time

  1. On the home screen, press MENU (Date), then +. You see Time.
  2. Press MENU. Change the hours (0–23).
  3. Press MENU. Change the minutes (0–59).
  4. Press MENU. Change the seconds (0–59).
  5. Press MENU once more. The cursor goes away.
  6. Press − (back to Date). Then press − again to save and go home.

How to set the exact time. The clock on this screen does not move while the menu is open. The new time is written when you go home.

  1. Set the time 1 minute ahead of the real time, with seconds 00. Example: real time is 13:04:20, so set 13:05:00.
  2. Press MENU until the cursor goes away. Press − once. You are on the Date screen.
  3. Watch a correct clock (for example your phone). At exactly 13:05:00, press −.

Seconds note. − stops at 01. To get 00, press + at 59.

6

Set a bell

There are 15 bells (Bell 01 to Bell 15). Each bell has 4 screens: time, days, duration and output. A bell rings only when all four are set.

How to find a bell

Open the menu (you are on Date). Then press + this many times:

Bell0102030405060708
Press +26101418222630
Bell09101112131415
Press +34384246505458

Each + moves one screen. The 4 screens of one bell come one after the other.

A. Bell time

The time the bell starts to ring.

  1. Press MENU. Change the hours.
  2. Press MENU. Change the minutes.
  3. Press MENU. The cursor goes away. Press + for the next screen.

B. Bell days

The days the bell rings. A * after a letter means yes, ring on this day. The letters are M T W T F S S (Monday to Sunday).

  1. Press MENU. The cursor is at Monday.
  2. Press + or − to put the * on, or to take it off.
  3. Press MENU to go to the next day. Do this for all 7 days.
  4. After Sunday, press MENU. The cursor goes away.

C. Bell duration

How long the bell rings.

  1. Press MENU. Change the number (0–239).
  2. Press MENU. Press + or − to switch between Seconds and Minutes.
  3. Press MENU. The cursor goes away.

Tip. A school bell usually rings 5 to 15 seconds. Do not use 0: the bell will ring for only about 1 second, or not at all.

D. Output relay

This connects the bell to the real bell. A* means the bell sounds. A without a star means it is silent.

  1. Press MENU. The cursor is at A.
  2. Press + or − to put the * on.
  3. Press MENU. The cursor goes away. Press + for the next bell.

Do not forget this screen. Without A*, the screen shows the bell ringing, but you hear nothing.

7

Save and exit

Your changes are saved when you leave the menu. There are two ways to leave:

Where you arePressWhat happens
Date screen (first screen)−Saves everything and goes home.
Output Relay 15 (last screen)+Saves everything and goes home.

The bell settings stay saved when the power is off. The clock keeps running on its own small battery.

Be careful.

If the power goes off while the menu is open, your new changes are lost.

Bells do not ring while the menu is open. If you open the menu while a bell is ringing, the bell keeps ringing until you go home. So change settings when no bell is due.

9

Turn a bell off

To stop a bell for a short time (for example in holidays), go to its Days screen and take off all the stars. Or go to its Output Relay screen and take off the star at A.

A new timer has all bells at 00:00 with no days. These bells never ring.

Problems and answers

ProblemCheck this
The bell did not ring.1. Is today marked with * on the Days screen?
2. Is A* on the Output Relay screen?
3. Is the weekday right on the Date screen?
4. Is the duration more than 0?
5. Is the timer on the home screen (menu closed)?
The screen shows ;A1 but there is no sound.Relay A is off for that bell. Or the bell wire is not connected.
The bell rings on the wrong days.The weekday is wrong. Set it on the Date screen (step 5 of Set the date).
The bell rings at the wrong time.Check the clock on the home screen. Check the 24-hour time: 2 PM is 14:00, not 02:00.
The time is wrong after a power cut.The clock battery is weak. Ask a technician to change it. Then set the date and time again.
My changes are gone.The power went off while the menu was open. Make the changes again and leave the menu to save.
+ changes a number but does not go to the next screen.The cursor is shown, so you are changing values. Press MENU until the cursor goes away.
The bell keeps ringing.Maybe the menu was opened while it rang. Go back to the home screen. Also check the unit: maybe it is Minutes, not Seconds.

Limits

SettingAllowed values
Number of bells15
Bell time00:00 to 23:59
Bell daysAny mix of Mon, Tue, Wed, Thu, Fri, Sat, Sun
Duration0 to 239, in Seconds or Minutes
Output1 relay (A)
DateDay 1–31, month 1–12, year 00–98 (2 digits)
Clock00:00:00 to 23:59:59 (24-hour)

A bell cannot ring past midnight. Example: a bell at 23:59 for 5 minutes stops at 00:00.
